Professor Paws exists because of one kid.

He learns differently, and homework was becoming a nightly battle.

I work in application security for a living, and I couldn't find a homework app that combined the kind of help he needed with the privacy and control I'd expect for a child.

So I started building one in the evenings. Professor Paws turns real schoolwork into small, manageable steps, with a puppy companion helping along the way.

Somewhere along the way, he started opening it without being asked. He even asked me to put his favorite pebble in the app. It's still there.

Questions parents ask

Does it give children the answer?

It's designed not to. It guides the method in small steps — visuals, manipulatives, hints — and breaks a step down smaller when your child is stuck, instead of revealing the answer.

If you ever see it slip, tell us — there's a report button right in the lesson.

What does the AI actually do?

The AI writes the lesson — pacing, wording, encouragement.

Arithmetic answers are checked by code, not by the AI, and every phonics letter sound is real recorded human speech. There's no open-ended AI chat anywhere in the app.

What data do you collect about my child?

The minimum a learning coach needs: a parent email, a nickname, your child's age, the schoolwork you enter (including any homework photos, which are processed and not stored in their profile), and learning progress.

Handwriting stays on the device. Nothing is sold, shared for ads, or used to train AI. Details in our Privacy Policy.

What ages and subjects?

Children roughly 8–13, met at the level they're actually working at. The beta covers spellings and phonics, times tables and number work, telling time, handwriting, and home reading.

Is a diagnosis required?

No. It's for children who learn differently or regularly find homework overwhelming — with or without a formal diagnosis.

Can my child make purchases?

No. The parent controls the account and any subscription. There is nothing a child can buy.

What device, and what will it cost?

Professor Paws is being built as an iPad app planned for the Apple App Store. Private beta families will be invited to test early before public launch.

The private beta is free. If we launch a paid subscription, you'll see the price before paying anything.

Does it replace school or tutoring?

No — it supports the work your child is already learning, and makes home practice calmer and more accessible.
