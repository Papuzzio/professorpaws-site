Does Professor Paws just give children answers?

No. It breaks the work into small steps and asks your child to do each one. When they're stuck, the help gets stronger a little at a time — a hint, then a smaller step, then something to move or count — and they finish the step themselves. How it works.

What ages is it for?

Children ages 8–13.

What subjects does it support?

Math — addition, subtraction, times tables, division, fractions, money, and telling time; spelling; and reading, including reading practice and comprehension questions grounded in the passage. It works from the homework your child already has, typed, pasted, or photographed.

How much is it?

$99 per student for one year.
