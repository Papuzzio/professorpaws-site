Professor Paws exists because of one kid.

He learns differently, and homework was becoming a nightly battle.

I work in application security for a living, and I couldn't find a homework app that combined the kind of help he needed with the privacy and control I'd expect for a child.

So I started building one in the evenings. Professor Paws turns real schoolwork into small, manageable steps, with a puppy companion helping along the way.

Somewhere along the way, he started opening it without being asked. He even asked me to put his favorite pebble in the app. It's still there.

We're now inviting a small number of families to test Professor Paws for free and help shape what comes next.
